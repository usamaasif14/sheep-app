# Privacy Policy for Sheep Farm Manager

**Last Updated:** October 2026

**Sheep Farm Manager** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is designed to assist farmers and livestock owners in managing sheep records, health logs, breeding schedules, and financial transactions. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal and farm data remains secure.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

1. **Farm & Livestock Data**:
   - Tag numbers, animal names, breeds, dates of birth, weight measurements, health logs, vaccination histories, breeding logs, and financial records.
   - This data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database and only synced to the cloud when you explicitly authenticate and perform a backup.

2. **Photos & Media (Camera and Gallery)**:
   - The App requests access to your camera and photo library solely to allow you to take and attach identification photos of your sheep. Photos remain on your device unless backed up to your personal private cloud account.

3. **Google Authentication & Cloud Backup**:
   - If you choose to enable Cloud Backup, we use Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Services to authenticate your account and securely store your farm database backups. We only access your basic account identifier (email/UID) to link your backup files.

---

### 2. Permissions Required by the App

- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**: To take photos of individual sheep for tag identification.
- **Storage / Media (`android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`)**: To select sheep photos from your device gallery.
- **Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)**: To send reminders for scheduled vaccinations, deworming, and estimated lambing dates.
- **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**: Required exclusively for optional cloud backup to Google Firebase. The App functions completely offline without internet connection.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Offline-First**: All your data is stored primarily on your physical device.
- **No Third-Party Advertising**: We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your livestock records, farm details, or financial numbers with any advertising networks or third parties.
- **Cloud Security**: Cloud backups are protected by Google Cloud infrastructure with industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion

- You retain full control over your data.
- You can delete any sheep, health record, or transaction at any time directly inside the App.
- To delete cloud backups, you can use the **Settings → Cloud Backup** screen in the App.
